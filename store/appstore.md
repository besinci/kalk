**Description:**

Sitting all day? Kalk (Turkish for "get up!") is a tiny menu bar app that reminds you to do 10 bodyweight squats after every 45 minutes of active computer use. One minute, no gym, no leaving your desk.

Why squats every 45 minutes? A 2024 study (Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports) found that breaking up 8.5 hours of sitting with 10 squats every 45 minutes lowered the blood-sugar response more than a single 30-minute walk. It was a small study of 18 men, so treat it as a nudge, not a promise.

Kalk only counts real computer time. The timer runs while you use the keyboard or mouse and pauses when you step away or the Mac sleeps, so only time at the desk counts. When it's time, a small window opens under the menu bar icon with a chime. It doesn't steal keyboard focus, so you can finish your sentence first. Click Done, Snooze or Skip.

An animated character does the reps with you and counts them. Stats show today against your daily goal, your streak, the last 7 days and every squat you've done.

- Counts only active time; pauses when you step away or the Mac sleeps
- Reminder that doesn't steal keyboard focus
- Done, Snooze or Skip in one click
- Animated character squats along and counts the reps
- Daily goal, streak, last 7 days and total squats
- Active hours and days, plus Pause for 1 hour or for today
- Adjustable interval, reps, goal, snooze and sound; optional launch at login
- Light and dark theme; drag the window anywhere, even to another display
- English and Turkish

Everything stays on your Mac: no account, no cloud, no tracking, no ads. Kalk only checks how long ago you last touched the keyboard or mouse, never what you typed.

## App Review notes

Kalk is a menu bar app with no Dock icon: click the squatting-figure icon in
the menu bar to open it. A reminder normally comes after 45 minutes of active
use; to see one quickly, open Settings (gear icon) and set "Break every" to
1 minute, then keep using the Mac for a minute. Launch at login is off by
default and can be turned on in Settings → General. No account or network
access is needed.
